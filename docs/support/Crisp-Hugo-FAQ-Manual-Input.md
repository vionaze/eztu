## Core product

### Q1
**Question:** What does EZTopUp sell?  
**Answer:** EZTopUp sells digital vouchers and e-vouchers for gaming and digital entertainment at eztopup.io. Some products are voucher codes; some are direct top-ups to a game account.

### Q2
**Question:** What is your website?  
**Answer:** Our website is https://eztopup.io

### Q3
**Question:** How do I buy a voucher?  
**Answer:** Open a product, choose a package and quantity, sign in (accept Terms), enter recipient email, then pay with crypto checkout (USDT/USDC). After payment is confirmed, fulfillment starts and you receive the voucher by email or secure link.

### Q4
**Question:** Do I need an account?  
**Answer:** Yes. You need to sign in before checkout. On the login page you must accept the Terms of Service first.

### Q5
**Question:** Which products are available?  
**Answer:** Please check https://eztopup.io/products or https://eztopup.io/vouchers for the live catalog. Availability can change.

## Payment (USDT / USDC)

### Q6
**Question:** What payment methods do you accept?  
**Answer:** Checkout is crypto-focused. We accept USDT and USDC stablecoins when enabled in checkout. Follow the on-screen payment instructions after you place the order.

### Q7
**Question:** Do you accept credit cards or bank transfer?  
**Answer:** Checkout is crypto-based (USDT/USDC). Card or local bank transfer is not the default method on the site.

### Q8
**Question:** Do you accept Bitcoin or Ethereum?  
**Answer:** Our main supported checkout assets are USDT and USDC stablecoins. Please use the payment options shown on the checkout page.

### Q9
**Question:** My crypto payment is pending / delayed. What should I do?  
**Answer:** Orders stay pending until the payment provider confirms the transaction on the network. Please wait for confirmations. If it stays pending for a long time, contact support with your order number and payment details at cs@eztopup.io.

### Q10
**Question:** Payment expired. What now?  
**Answer:** If the payment window expired before confirmation, place a new order and complete payment within the new window. Contact cs@eztopup.io if you already sent funds after expiry and share proof.

## Delivery & vouchers

### Q11
**Question:** How do I receive my voucher?  
**Answer:** After payment is confirmed, a secure voucher link or code is delivered to the recipient email you entered, and may also appear in your purchase history when signed in.

### Q12
**Question:** How long does delivery take?  
**Answer:** Delivery starts after payment confirmation. Most voucher fulfillments complete shortly after confirmation, but network or supplier delays can happen. If payment is confirmed and you still have no code, contact support with your order number.

### Q13
**Question:** Where is my voucher code?  
**Answer:** Check the recipient email (including spam), your purchase history on the site, and any secure voucher link we sent. Share your order number with support if you cannot find it after payment confirmation.

### Q14
**Question:** Can I get a refund after I received the voucher code?  
**Answer:** Once a voucher code has been delivered, the transaction is complete and generally non-refundable, because codes are issued live from our suppliers. If payment failed or our system did not deliver after a confirmed payment, contact cs@eztopup.io for a review.

### Q15
**Question:** I lost my voucher code. Can you resend it?  
**Answer:** We can try to resend delivery information for your order if it was fulfilled. Contact cs@eztopup.io with your order number and account email. We cannot reverse a code that was already redeemed elsewhere.

### Q16
**Question:** I entered the wrong email.  
**Answer:** Contact cs@eztopup.io as soon as possible with the order number, the email you used, and the correct email. If the code was already opened or redeemed, options may be limited.

## Mobile Legends / top-up

### Q17
**Question:** Do I need a game ID for all products?  
**Answer:** No. Voucher products usually only need your email. Direct top-up products such as Mobile Legends require User ID and Zone/Server ID.

### Q18
**Question:** How do I top up Mobile Legends?  
**Answer:** Open the Mobile Legends product, choose a diamond package, enter your ML User ID and Zone/Server ID carefully, sign in, pay with USDT/USDC, then wait for confirmation. Diamonds go to the account details you entered—not as a voucher code.

### Q19
**Question:** I entered the wrong Mobile Legends ID / Zone.  
**Answer:** Top-ups to an incorrect User ID or Zone may not be reversible. Contact cs@eztopup.io immediately with order number and screenshots. Prevention: always double-check ID and Zone before paying.

### Q23
**Question:** Where is the Privacy Policy?  
**Answer:** https://eztopup.io/privacy

### Q24
**Question:** Why must I accept Terms before login?  
**Answer:** To use EZTopUp you must agree to our Terms of Service, including that delivered voucher codes finalize the order.

### Q25
**Question:** Can I cancel an order?  
**Answer:** Before payment confirmation, you may simply not complete payment. After payment confirms and especially after a voucher code is delivered, cancellation/refund is generally not available except for verified system issues reviewed by support.
